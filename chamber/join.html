Benefits:

  • Free listing in the member directory
  • Access to basic networking events
  • Monthly newsletter subscription
  • Basic business workshops (2 per year)
  • Digital member badge for website

Cost: Free for qualified non-profit organizations

Bronze Membership

Benefits:

  • All NP Membership benefits
  • Priority listing in the member directory
  • Access to all networking events (member price)

Benefits:

  • Personalized business development plan
  • Free booth at annual business exposition
