Site Name

FarmCrib

Name: FarmCrib

Optional Domain: farmcrib.com.ng

Reason for Selection: The name "FarmCrib" combines "Farm" to represent the agricultural focus and "Crib" to symbolize a safe, nurturing space where farmers can find everything they need—buyers for their produce, weather information for better decisions, and equipment for rent. The name is memorable, brandable, and immediately communicates the platform's purpose of being a central hub for farming resources in Nigeria.

Site Purpose

FarmCrib provides a comprehensive online marketplace that directly connects Nigerian farmers with buyers, offering weather-based farming tips, and listing agricultural equipment for rent. The platform addresses three critical challenges in Nigerian agriculture:

  • Market Access: Enables small-scale farmers to reach urban and regional buyers without middlemen
  • Information Access: Delivers weather-informed advice and crop recommendations for major farming regions
  • Resource Access: Makes agricultural equipment affordable through a rental marketplace

The site serves as a digital bridge between rural farming communities and urban markets, empowering economic growth through direct connections, knowledge sharing, and resource accessibility.

Scenarios

Scenario 1: Farmer Seeking Buyers

"I am a small-scale farmer in Kaduna with a surplus of tomatoes. How do I list my produce on FarmCrib and find buyers in Lagos who are willing to pay a fair price?"

FarmCrib allows farmers to create product listings with photos, descriptions, prices, and location. Buyers can browse by category, location, and price. The platform provides direct contact information for seamless transactions between farmers and buyers.

Scenario 2: Farmer Seeking Equipment

"I need to prepare my farmland for the upcoming rainy season but cannot afford to buy a tractor. What agricultural equipment is available for rent near me in Oyo State, and how much does it cost per day?"

FarmCrib's equipment rental section lists available machinery with pricing, availability calendars, and owner contact details. Farmers can search by equipment type, location, and rental period to find affordable options that fit their needs and budget.

Scenario 3: Buyer Seeking Fresh Produce

"I run a restaurant in Abuja and want to source fresh vegetables directly from farmers. Where can I find verified farmers selling organic produce at wholesale prices?"

Buyers can browse FarmCrib's marketplace by category (vegetables, fruits, grains), filter by location and price, and view farmer profiles with ratings. The platform connects restaurants and retailers directly with farmers, cutting out intermediaries and ensuring fresher produce.

Color Usage

  • Earth Green (#2D5016): Primary color for headings, navigation bar, section titles, and logo elements. Represents growth, agriculture, and stability.
  • Harvest Gold (#D4A017): Secondary color for buttons, links, call-to-action elements, and highlighted content. Represents prosperity, harvest, and value.
  • Sky Blue (#87CEEB): Accent color for weather-related sections, information icons, and background highlights. Represents clarity, weather data, and trust.
  • White (#FFFFFF): Background for content areas and cards to ensure readability.
  • Dark Brown (#3E2723): Body text color for optimal contrast and readability.

Typography Notes

  • Merriweather (Serif): Used for all headings (h1-h6) and the site logo to convey tradition, reliability, and agricultural heritage. The serif style adds authority and readability for section titles.
  • Open Sans (Sans-serif): Used for all body text, navigation menus, buttons, form labels, and UI elements. Its clean, modern appearance ensures excellent readability across devices and screen sizes.
  • Font weights range from 400 (regular) to 700 (bold) for both typefaces, providing clear hierarchy.
  • Line height is set to 1.6 for body text and 1.2 for headings to optimize reading experience.
