About FarmCrib

Our Mission

FarmCrib was founded with a singular vision: to bridge the gap between Nigerian farmers and buyers through technology. Nigeria has over 30 million smallholder farmers who produce incredible agricultural products, yet many struggle to access fair markets, reliable pricing, and modern equipment.

Our marketplace directly connects farmers to buyers across Nigeria's 36 states, eliminating costly middlemen and ensuring farmers receive fair compensation for their hard work. We also provide weather-informed farming advice and equipment rentals to help farmers increase productivity.
